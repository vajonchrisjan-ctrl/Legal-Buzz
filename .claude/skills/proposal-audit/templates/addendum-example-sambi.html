Kah Lawyers proves it

Lawyer, not agent

AI often sends immigration questions to the migration agents register. Sambi's pages should explain plainly why a lawyer can go further than an agent, at a tribunal or in court, so AI has a reason to recommend a firm like Sambi.

E · What it adds to the plan
E · What this adds to your plan

Five additions to the growth plan, in the order we'd do them.

These slot into the Analyse, Fix, Build and Buzz steps in your proposal. They don't replace anything, and they run alongside your current SEO provider.

Claim the reputation listings AI already reads

Google Business Profiles for Brisbane and Melbourne with reviews flowing in, Law Society listings, and a Best Lawyers submission. These are the sites AI cites next to Kah Lawyers and rarely next to you.

Build the Visa Guidance and Refusals hubs first

Visa guidance is the largest topic (39,829 questions) and tribunal representation is your core work. Both hubs go out under Tanguy's name, with real processing times, costs and refusal reasons.

Fill the eight missing topics

Partner and de facto visas, protection visas, character and s501, processing times and policy changes. Most come from merging the duplicate pages you already have.

Get beyond Google's AI Overviews

Short YouTube explainers from Tanguy, plus a presence where clients ask each other (Reddit and Facebook groups), so Perplexity, AI Mode and ChatGPT find independent proof, not just your website.

Track citations and names every month

We'll report both measures side by side. Names: how often AI says "Sambi Legal". Citations: how often AI links to sambilegal.com.au. Both are tracked against Hansen Migration, Kah Lawyers and Katsaros.
