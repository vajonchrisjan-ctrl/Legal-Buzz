Six questions to open the call

Each of these blocks a decision we cannot make for you. We have deliberately not guessed at any of them, and nothing in this document asserts an answer.

1Offices

Do you trade from Moss Vale?

Your LinkedIn page lists a second office at 348–354 Argyle Street, Moss Vale NSW 2577. Your website names only Bowral, on every page. If Moss Vale is live, it currently has no page, no address and no local signal anywhere.
2Brands

What is "The HR Brief" for?

It has been a registered business name against your ABN since 26 June 2024 and has no web presence we can find. Is it trading, dormant, or planned? (We checked hrbrief.com.au — it is an unrelated newsletter, not yours.)
3Google profile

Who holds your Google Business Profile?

We could not verify it from outside. That is not the same as saying it does not exist — we need someone signed in to confirm it, because for a single-town firm it is the highest-value asset on this list.
4Founding date

Which year is right?

The register shows your ABN active from 31 Aug 2015. LinkedIn says founded 2016. Localsearch says "trading for 10 years". We are not calling any of them wrong — but we will not put a "since" claim in front of clients until you confirm which one it is.
5Team

How many people, and who is client-facing?

Your About page names nine. LinkedIn says 2–10. LawTap tells searchers you have "1 Solicitor". Your own page is almost certainly the right one — but the directories are telling a different story.
6Measurement

Has analytics ever been installed?

We found none — no GA4, no Tag Manager, no pixel. If there was a property once, recovering its history changes what we can show you as a baseline. If not, the baseline starts the day we install it.
