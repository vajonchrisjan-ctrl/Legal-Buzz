Where demand actually sits

Not measured — and we will not guess

We have no search volume data for your services or your towns, and no ranking data for any phrase.

Both require a keyword tool and a rank tracker, and neither was connected for this round. We will not tell you that "conveyancing Bowral gets N searches a month" — we did not measure it, and a guess is indistinguishable from a measurement once printed. Establishing this is the first line item in Phase 1 (page {{P7}}). A missing volume means unknown, never zero, and overlapping phrase volumes are never added together.

04
How AI has changed search

An assistant can only recommend what it can read.

We could not test what the assistants say about you. We could test what they would find if they looked — and that part is measurable.

What we did not test, and why

We did not run discovery or branded questions on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overview or AI Mode.

Doing it properly needs a neutral research account per platform, a fresh session each, and an identical question set. A personal signed-in account returns a personalised answer, which measures nothing. None was available, so no mention rate, citation share or "AI visibility score" appears in this document. When we run it, the result is a sample mention rate per platform — never market share.

The pattern this produces

A model asked "who is Signature Law?" has enough to answer. A model asked "who should I use for conveyancing in Bowral?" has almost nothing to match you on.

That gap between branded recall and discovery recall is the normal failure mode for a firm with good people and unmarked-up pages — and it is the part that closes fastest, because it is markup and description rather than reputation.
