How to read this
This is our assessment against what we measured, not an industry-standard index and not a Google metric. It exists so the same nine categories can be re-scored in 90 days against the same evidence and the movement can be seen.

Bar colour carries no information the number does not — every bar carries its own value. We tested the navy / blue / red palette under protan, deutan and tritan simulation: every pair stays at ΔE ≥ 55. Blue and red differ in hue rather than lightness, which is why the value labels matter.
