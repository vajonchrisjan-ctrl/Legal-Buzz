How we'd deliver it

Fix, build, then earn trust and measure.

Three phases over six months. The first needs almost nothing from you.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4

Fix

Analytics and Search Console live. Ranking baseline. 45 meta descriptions, homepage H1, heading structure, LegalService schema, tel: links, Google Business Profile claimed.

Outcome: you can see what is happening, and every page describes itself.
Phase 2 · Weeks 5–14

Build

Service-page mobile LCP under 2.5 s. Three thinnest practice areas deepened. Social cards, /llms.txt, question-shaped sections, privacy policy, accessibility fixes, listings corrected.

Outcome: the pages are worth arriving at.
Phase 3 · Weeks 15–26

Earn trust & measure

Publishing restarted in employment law. Reviews. Backlink and AI-visibility baselines. Re-score of all nine categories against the same evidence.

Outcome: a second data point, and proof of movement or its absence.
